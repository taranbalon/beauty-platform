\# Design \& Taste Standards for UI



\## Core Philosophy

\- Avoid default AI-generated aesthetics: no random purple/blue mesh gradients, no bloated card borders, no generic centered landing hero templates.

\- Favor restrained, high-craft minimalism: subtle borders over deep shadows, intentional negative space, clear typographic hierarchy.



\## Typography \& Color

\- Never use pure black (#000000) for text on light backgrounds; use slate/zinc shades (#0f172a, #18181b).

\- Tighten tracking on display headings (tracking-tight); keep body copy comfortable (tracking-normal).

\- Monospace labels, badges and numerical readouts should be sized down (text-xs) with medium/semi-bold weight.



\## Layout \& Spacing

\- Maintain strict padding and margin rhythm (multiples of 4px/8px).

\- Border radius hierarchy: nested inner elements must have smaller radiuses than their parent containers.

\- Micro-borders: prefer subtle translucent borders over heavy drop shadows.



\## Interactions

\- Fast, subtle transitions (150ms–200ms ease-out).

\- Explicit hover, focus-visible, and active states on every interactive element.
